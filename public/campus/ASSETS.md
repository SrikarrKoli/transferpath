# Campus 3D assets

Landmark halls (Clock Tower, Counselor, Library, Classrooms, Registrar, Dorms, Rec, Union) are **custom authored meshes** in `campus-landmarks.ts` — unique campus silhouettes, not Kenney tract houses or grey offices.

Kenney.nl CC0 kits still supply **life and props**: people, cars, palms, parasols, flags, pier, planters, lights.

**License (Kenney packs):** [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/) — public domain. Attribution is not required; we credit Kenney because the packs are excellent.
